26. The biggest design principle for your Replit prototype

I would tell Claude/Replit not to interpret screens 2–18 as 18 separate navigation items.

Instead:

Main navigation

Home

My Care

Appointments

Profile

And perhaps:

Messages/Care

Then:

Inside My Care → Consultation

You have:

Overview | Chat | 3D | Reports | Imaging | Treatment | Physio

That is where your teammate's point about the thread comes in.

The document's own Screen 7 is already showing that the consultation thread contains things like the AI summary, doctor conversation, and doctor-approved lab summary.

So yes, you were right to question why 3D, reports, imaging etc. would be completely separate pages. They can be separate views/tabs within the same consultation, which is a much better UX.
